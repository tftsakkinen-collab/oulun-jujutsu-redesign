What to Bring to Your First Class in Oulu

Attending your first class at Äimäkuja 6 A is made completely effortless. Here is what to pack in your bag:

1. Indoor Sportswear

A standard t-shirt and sweatpants (without zippers or metal buttons). Gi uniforms are acquired during the course.

2. Flip-Flops / Sandals

Sandals for walking between the changing rooms and the edge of the tatami mats. Training on mats is done barefoot.

3. Water Bottle & Towel

Fresh water bottle. Showers and foot washing stations are available at our academy prior to entering the tatami.

4. Clean Hygiene & Trimmed Nails

Trimmed finger and toe nails ensure safe, injury-free training for you and your practice partner.

Dojo Glossary

Budo & Hokutoryu Terminology

You will easily learn these terms during your beginner course!
Rei (礼) Etiquette

Bow. Expression of mutual respect for the dojo, instructor, and training partner.

Hajime (始め) Command

Begin! Instructor command to start a technique or drill.

Yame (止め) Command

Stop! Immediately freeze and listen to the instructor's feedback.

Dojo (道場) Venue

Training hall. Our academy is located at Äimäkuja 6 A in Oulu.

Sensei (先生) Title

Teacher / Coach. Traditional respectful title for a martial arts master.

Senpai (先輩) Role

Senior student who guides and assists beginners during training.

Ukemi (受身) Skill

Breakfall technique / safe landing. Taught right from week 1 of the beginner course.

Tori & Uke (取り・受け) Partners

Tori: Defender (defender). Uke: Attacker (receiver of technique).

Gi / Dogi (道着) Gear

Official Ju-Jutsu suit (regular T-shirt & sweatpants are fine for beginners).

Obi (帯) Belt

Belt indicating practitioner rank and progress (Yellow ➔ Black Belt).

Tatami (畳) Mat

Cushioned martial arts mat. Practice is always barefoot with clean feet.

Seiza (正座) Etiquette

Formal kneeling posture used for bowing and focus at the start and end of practice.

Belt Progression Pathway

From Yellow Belt to Black Belt Mastery

In Hokutoryu Ju-Jutsu you progress through structured, nationally certified belt levels at your own pace:

5th KYU

Yellow Belt

Beginner course exam. Covers foundational strikes, blocks, releases, and basic throws.

4th KYU

Orange Belt

Intermediate releases, sweeps, ground defense, and combination techniques.

3rd KYU

Green Belt

Advanced joint locks, weaponless defenses, applications, and stand-up sparring.

2nd KYU

Blue Belt

Advanced tactical self-defense, armed threat defenses, and applied combat skills.

1st KYU

Brown Belt

Mastery-level combinations, high speed, and assistant instructing before Black Belt.

1st DAN+

Black Belt

Mastery and instructorship recognized nationwide by Hokutoryu Ju-Jutsu Finland.

Beginner Guide

First Time on the Mat?

Taking your first step is easier than you think! No prior athletic background or special gear is needed.

T-Shirt & Sweatpants are Perfect

No martial arts uniform (Gi) required! You can train in comfortable sportswear (t-shirt & sweatpants/shorts) for the majority of the beginner course – uniform is only needed near the belt test.

Barefoot & Sandals

We train barefoot on the clean mat area. Bring flip-flops or sandals for walking in the corridors and changing rooms.

Hygiene & Safety

Trim your fingernails and toenails short before class. Remove rings, watches, and jewelry for safety on the mats.

Water Bottle

Bring a refillable water bottle. Regular water breaks are provided throughout class to keep you hydrated.
